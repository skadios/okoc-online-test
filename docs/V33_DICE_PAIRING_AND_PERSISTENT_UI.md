# V33 — Individual dice, Black Plague pairing and persistent notifications

- Multi-player dice are now server-staged: playing the card only starts the action; each participating player must explicitly click their own die.
- Meat for Meat, Mad King and Black Plague no longer roll every die from one player’s click.
- Black Plague now follows the printed rule: all Nobles choose their pairs; if there is an odd number of Nobles, the remaining Noble rolls two dice and loses 200 gold only if at least one of those two dice is 1.
- Pairing is interactive and the chat remains available while the decision window is open, so players can discuss pairings before confirming.
- Dice result notifications remain until the X is pressed.
- Generic game notifications and card-event notifications no longer auto-disappear after a timer; they remain until manually closed.
- Multi-roll notifications explicitly say when they are still waiting for the other players instead of prematurely declaring the final effect.

The Black Plague implementation follows the supplied Print-at-Home card wording on page 23 of the source PDF.
