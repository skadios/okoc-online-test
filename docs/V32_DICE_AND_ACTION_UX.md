# V32 — Dice and action UX

- Dice cards now require an explicit click before the server rolls.
- Dice results are persistent until the player closes the result with `×`.
- Dice results include the corresponding player name below each die.
- Multi-player dice events expose player-labelled results.
- Sub Rosa keeps its printed role-specific behavior and now waits for an explicit die roll after the hand/hidden-Knight choice.
- We Ride Together now resolves in stages: both selected Nobles roll, ties require another roll, the lower roller chooses between paying 200 or proposing 100 each, and the higher roller accepts/refuses the split proposal.
- Betray the King, People’s Champion and Scapegoat tie-break rolls also wait for an explicit die click.
- Action/card explanations remain visible until closed with `×` instead of disappearing on a timer.
- A fixed chat dock remains usable above game decision overlays.
