# Card assets

The 42 physical card designs use the supplied print-and-play artwork, recropped to a consistent 246×346 display size. English and French assets are provided separately under `client/public/assets/cards/` and `client/public/assets/cards/fr/`.

The physical cards are intentionally kept in their original black/white/gray appearance. The red/black/white treatment belongs to the surrounding web UI and digital material, not to the physical card artwork.
